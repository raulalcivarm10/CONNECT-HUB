# ConnectHub

**Institution & Event Operations Hub**

Run your institutions, venues, events, attendance and revenue from one cloud console — connected to your registration and check-in app.

`Category: Event Management` · `B2B SaaS` · `Venue Operations`

---

## Overview

ConnectHub is the administrative console institutions use to operate their events end to end. Model your physical spaces, schedule events without clashes, track who attended, and watch revenue come in — all from a single, role-based panel available 24/7 in the cloud. Your attendees register and check in through your mobile app; ConnectHub gives your team and your directors the control room behind it.

---

## Features

- **Full venue hierarchy** — venues, halls, sub-halls and event layouts, with capacity validation.
- **Conflict-free scheduling** — automatic detection of time and space overlaps, including setup and cleanup windows.
- **Executive dashboards** — attendance and revenue in real time, with drill-down to the detail.
- **Multi-institution & roles** — every team sees only its module; one super admin oversees them all.
